The third stop of the ISU World Cup Short Track begins Friday December 7. Don’t miss any of the action – including the first day of qualifying rounds – which will be streamed live to all fans on Skating ISU’sYouTube. Make sure you subscribe to the channel to receive live stream notifications.

Suzanne Schulting (NED) 2018©International Skating Union (ISU)
The live action will continue on Saturday December 8 and Sunday December 9 with more exclusive World Cup sessions. Some geo-restrictions will apply on YouTube for Days 2 and 3 of Almaty, but if you find yourself unable to watch the stream, check out one of the following channels to get access:
SBSfor those watching from the Republic of Korea.
ISU Skating Channelfor everyone else, where you can also watch for free.
The full schedule for ISU World Cup Short Track Almaty is outlined below; clear your agenda and make time to enjoy the fastest sport on ice!
