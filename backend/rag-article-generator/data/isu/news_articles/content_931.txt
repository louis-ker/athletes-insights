Today the International Skating Union launches the ISU Skating Channel.  The first Korean Air ISU World Cup Short Track Event of the season will be available as a live stream through the Channel.  Please note that some countries might be geo-blocked due to television right restrictions.  The ISU Skating Channel is available here:http://livemanager.eurovision.edgesuite.net/isu/site/index.html
The ISU will continue to search for online solutions to bring ISU Events to the fans and the skating family without infringing the television contracts in place.
We are also pleased to announce that the ISU has opened a new Facebook page for Short Track Speed Skating. The page is aimed for those who have an interest in the sport and wish to follow the season and share their experiences through social media.  The page is available here:www.facebook.com/ISUShortTrackSpeedSkating
For the latest information on Short Track Speed Skating Events and to find out which Events will be available through the ISU Skating Channel, we invite you to join the International Skating Union and ISU Short Track Speed Skating Facebook pages.
Last August the ISU opened the International Skating Union and the ISU Figure Skating Facebook pages and at the beginning of November we will open the ISU Speed Skating page.
