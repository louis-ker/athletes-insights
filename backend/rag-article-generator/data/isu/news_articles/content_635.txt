#ShortTrackSkating                           #WorldShortTrack

Charles Hamelin (CAN)
The crowd roared from start to finish asCharles Hamelin(CAN) led from the front to take home gold in the Men’s1000m A Finalon Sunday at the World Short Track Championships in Montreal, Canada.
It’s his 11thWorld Championship gold and 34thmedal overall.
Hamelin held strong throughout the race, increasing the gap to his nearest opponent with each lap until the end, when South Korea’sLim Hyo Junstormed back to make it a photo finish, coming in second in 1:22.283, compared to Hamelin’s 1:22.249.
Sjinkie Knegt(NED) won bronze.
Hwang Dae Hon(KOR) fell well behind to finish fourth whileSamuel Girardwas penalized for the second day in a row.
