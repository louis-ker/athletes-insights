While everyone is focused on the Short Track World Tour this weekend, the O word is looming large in the minds of our racers. It’s Olympic season, and that means pure excitement. From established Olympic legends like Choi Minjeong of the Korean White Tigers to William Dandjinou of the Canadian Ice Maples, who is anticipating his first Games at Milano Cortina 2026, they’re all planning to make this the greatest season of their careers.
