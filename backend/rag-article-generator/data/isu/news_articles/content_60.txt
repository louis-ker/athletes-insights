
In an extraordinary display of skill, unity, and determination, Team China excelled during the 2024/25 Short Track season, securing an impressive collection of gold medals. Led by champions likeLiu ShaoangandSun Long, the team thrived on preparation, motivation, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.
From historic victories at theISU World Short Track Championships 2024in Rotterdam to emotional triumphs on home ice in Beijing, each race highlighted both their individual brilliance and the strength of their collective spirit.  As Liu Shaoang powerfully expressed, they "get on ice as one, and get off as one," showcasing an unbreakable bond and shared pride in their collective achievements.
Guided by Head Coach Zhang Jing’s powerful philosophy, "One goal, one heart," Team China found their secret to success in unity, relentless preparation, and resilience. Every race was a celebration of their shared dedication, where individual victories became collective triumphs.
Competing in front of a passionate home crowd at Beijing’s historic Capital Indoor Stadium, the team drew energy from the fans, driven by pride and a desire to represent their country. For Team China, the season was about more than medals; it was a powerful demonstration of the incredible force of teamwork and passion.
