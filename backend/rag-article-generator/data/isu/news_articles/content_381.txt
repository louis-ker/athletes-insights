#ShortTrackSkating
The ISU has decided to hold a series of Online Video Seminars for ISU Officials in Short Track Speed Skating, which will be organized by the ISU Short Track Speed Skating Technical Committee using the Zoom Communication software as follows:
Saturday August 21, 2021 at 13h00-15h30 Central European Time (CET)
Sunday August 22, 2021 at 13h00-15h30 Central European Time (CET)
Saturday August 28, 2021 at 13h00-15h30 Central European Time (CET)
Sunday August 29, 2021 at 13h00-15h30 Central European Time (CET)

©International Skating Union (ISU)
Saturday August 21, 2021: For Referees, Starters and Competitor Stewards
Sunday August 22, 2021: For Referees and Competitor Stewards only
Saturday August 28, 2021: For Referees only
Sunday August 29, 2021: For Referees only
The seminars will be conducted in English.
These seminars will be open to ISU Referees, ISU Starters and ISU Competitors Stewards as listed in ISU Communication no. 2394 for season 2021/22.
In addition, Referees, Starters and Competitors Stewards listed as International Officials marked with “W” (See Rule 289, paragraph 5.e) in ISU Communication No. 2394 may also participate.
Other International Officials may attend as observers provided their attendance is approved by the Short Track Speed Skating Technical Committee. Request for attendance of such observers must be included in the entries.
Sessions will be recorded and made accessible to participating ISU and International “W” Officials for use in future Regional and National Seminars.
Please send your entries by email not later thanJuly 31, 2021to:
Nathalie Lambert, Chair ISU Short Track Speed Skating Technical Committee
nathalie.lambert@isu.org
Fabrice Prahin, ISU Sports Manager Speed Skating
prahin@isu.ch
When entering an Official to the seminars, the email address of the Official must be indicated in order to send the online meeting invitation.
Invitation and login details to attend the seminars will be sent to all invited participants by email before the seminar.
Participation in the relevant seminars will be considered as participation in an ISU Course in accordance with the requirements for ISU Officials in Rule 289, paragraph 6.a).
Please read full ISU Communication 2401.
