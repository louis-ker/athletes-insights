
In an unforgettable season marked by resilience and determination,Kristen Santos Griswoldrose above challenges to claim the prestigious Crystal Globe the ultimate recognition for the top-performing female and male skater of the season. From the early nerves at the first World Tour event and a challenging weekend with multiple falls, Kristen transformed setbacks into powerful fuel for her inspiring comeback. Her triumphant victories in Seoul and Milan showcased remarkable mental strength, as she turned moments of doubt into golden performances, finally standing atop the podium with a sense of accomplishment and pride.
Kristen’s journey was not just a story of personal achievement, it was a testament to the strength found in adversity and the unwavering support of those around her. Each gold medal, including her thrilling back-to-back victories, was earned through resilience, adaptability, and a refusal to surrender to pressure. Kristen proudly carried the hopes and dreams of Team USA Eagle, sharing each emotional victory with coaches, family, and teammates. Her Crystal Globe win epitomized the spirit of Short Track: fierce competition, powerful emotion, and the incredible joy of perseverance.
