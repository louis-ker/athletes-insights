The Short Track Skating season came to a climatic end at the KB Financial Group ISU World Short Track Championships 2023 in Seoul (KOR) where newly crowned Crystal Globe Trophy winnerPark Ji Wonskated to two golds on home ice.
In addition to Park's achievements, four-time gold medalistXandra Velzeboer(NED) as well asSuzanne Schulting(NED),Pietro Sighel(ITA) andLin Xiaojun(CHN) also lit up the venue, which was full of excited and emotional fans. Be sure to watch ourrecapnow!
