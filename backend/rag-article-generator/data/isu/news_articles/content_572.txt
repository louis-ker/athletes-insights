The ISU World Cup Short Track Series continues this weekend in Dresden, Germany. Don’t miss any of the action from the fourth leg of this thrilling event – including the first day of qualifying rounds on Friday February 1 – which will be streamed live to all fans on theISU Skating YouTube page. Make sure you subscribe to the channel to receive live stream notifications.

EnergieVerbund Arena - Dresden (GER) ©International Skating Union (ISU)
The live action will continue on Saturday February 2 and Sunday February 3 with more exclusive World Cup sessions. Some geo-restrictions will apply on YouTube for Days 2 and 3 of Dresden, but if you find yourself unable to watch the stream, check out one of the following channels to get access:
SBSfor those watching from the Republic of Korea.
ISU Skating Channelfor everyone else, where you can also watch for free.
