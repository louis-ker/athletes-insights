#ShortTrackSkating
As announced inISU Communication No. 2548, the ISU Council approved the establishment, as from the season 2023/24, of an ISU Junior World Cup Short Track Series, funded through the ISU Development Program and replacing the current Short Track Junior Challenge Series.
The ISU now invites European ISU Members interested to host a Junior World Cup Short Track Competition to submit their bid application to the ISU not later than May 20, 2023.
