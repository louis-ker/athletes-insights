The ISU publishes the general conditions, racing rules and entry requirements for the inaugural season of the ISU Junior World Cup Short Track Series.
The ISU Junior World Cup Short Track Speed Skating 2023/24 will be presented under the title “ISU Junior World Cup Short Track” and a specific logo. For the season 2023/24 the ISU has designated the following competitions as “Junior World Cup Competitions”:Leeuwarden NED November 25-26, 2023Leeuwarden NED December 2-3, 2023Pending TBC February 17-18, 2024
Find out all details and conditions of the ISU World Cup Short Track Speed Skating 2022/23 inISU Communication 2579.
