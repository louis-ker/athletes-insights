Future stars of Short Track take center stage at the ISU World Junior Short Track Speed Skating Championships.

ISU World Junior Short Track Speed Skating Championships 2017©International Skating Union (ISU)
The 2019 World Junior Short Track Speed Skating Championships begin Friday January 25 in the bustling city of Montreal, Canada. The best Junior Skaters will battle on the ice to make their name known in the sport.
We’re bringing you the action LIVE and for FREE on our YouTube channel:Skating ISU. No matter where you are in the world, make sure you clear your agenda, sit down and sign in on Saturday and Sunday to witness the next generation of Short Track talent.
