Rotterdam Ahoy venue - ISU World Short Track Speed Skating Championships 2017 in Rotterdam (NED) © ISU
The International Skating Union (ISU) is proud to announce Nederlandse Loterij (Dutch Lottery) as the official title sponsor for the highly anticipated ISU World Short Track Speed Skating Championships 2024, scheduled to take place in the vibrant city of Rotterdam from March 15-17, 2024. This partnership represents a significant milestone in the world of Short Track Speed Skating and promises to deliver an unforgettable sporting spectacle.
TheNederlandse Loterij ISU World Short Track Championships 2024are the pinnacle of Short Track Speed Skating, bringing together the world's finest athletes to compete for glory. The event will take place at the renowned Rotterdam Ahoy Arena, which has a rich history of hosting world-class sporting events. With the support of Nederlandse Loterij, this edition promises to be the most exciting and dynamic championships yet.
Nederlandse Loterij is a leading organization dedicated to generating funds for a variety of charitable causes in the Netherlands. They have been a long-standing supporter of sports and culture, contributing to the development of local communities and fostering talent. Partnering with the ISU for the World Short Track Championships underscores Nederlandse Loterij's commitment to promoting active and healthy lifestyles and supporting emerging talents.
"These Championships are going to be a spectacle full of amazing sports performances and entertainment. The ISU is very excited to partner with Nederlandse Loterij as they have been a strong supporter of skating," said Jae Youl Kim, ISU President. "Together with the world’s best Short Track Skaters, we will create an electric atmosphere and let fans enjoy the sport all over the world."
"As the biggest supporter of sports in the Netherlands, the Nederlandse Loterij is proud that, as title sponsor, we have been able to contribute to bringing a major international Short Track World Championships to the Netherlands. We look forward to another spectacular event in Ahoy Rotterdam.”  said Joost Zuure, Head of sponsorships & beneficiaries at Nederlandse Loterij.
The Championships promise to be a thrilling experience for both athletes and fans, andNederlandse Loterij's support will undoubtedly contribute to its success. The event will be held from March 15-17, 2024, and some tickets are still available for purchasehere.
