
Ye Jin Kim and Suk Hee Shim (KOR)
Already dominant in individual events, South Korea’s Ladies Short Track skaters proved they were equally strong as a unit, skating to gold in theLadies’ 3000-metre Relay Final.
Shim Suk Hee,Kim A Lang,Choi Min Jeong, andKim Ye Jin(KOR) combined to finish ahead of the Netherlands, who claimed just their second medal of the weekend.
It’s also been a disappointing World Championship for Canada’s Ladies but the relay quartet ofValerie Maltais,Marianne St-Gelais,Kasandra BradetteandKim Boutinmade up for it somewhat by claiming bronze.
“I knew the crowd would be supportive regardless of the result and I felt that today,”St-Gelaissaid.
Canada skated out to an early lead, staying ahead of the pack for the first 10 laps. Korea followed behind before pulling ahead with 15 laps remaining, showing its dominance by widening the gap with each lap.
“I’m really happy for our team,”Shimsaid.
