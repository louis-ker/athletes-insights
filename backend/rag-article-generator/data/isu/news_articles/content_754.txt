In accordance with ISU Rule 289, paragraph 6.b), the ISU has decided to hold a course for ISU Referees, ISU Starters and ISU Competitors Stewards in Short Track Speed Skating, which will be organized by the ISU in Calgary, Canada on October 5-9, 2016.
