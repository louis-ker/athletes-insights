ISU CoE Heereveen Welcoming Short Trackers of the World
@KNSB
The ISU Center of Excellence in Heerenveen is organizing a series of Short Track training camps during the season 2022/2023, offering their expertise, experience and facilities to the Short Track family of the world.There are 7 Training Camps each consisting of one-week period, starting from September 26, 2022 till March 23, 2023. Skaters can choose from minimum 1-week to the full 7-week program including ice training, strength & conditioning and numerous workshops about material-set up, explanation of the new ISU Special & Technical rules, etc.There are minimum requirements for the participants as follows:• 500m personal best of 46 seconds (Women) 43 seconds (Men).• Proof of full medical and personal liability insurance prior to arrival.• Minimum: 18 years old.• Accompanied by a CoachThe registration is open via http://pay-ce.com/ directly with the Heerenveen CoE. There is a maximum number of twenty skaters allowed per Training Camp. Registration will be based on a first come first served basis. For further information, please refer to the enclosed flyer.Other ISU Centers of Excellence also offer regular training camps for different levels of Skaters and Coaches. Please check their websites and get updated with their excellent training programs that might suit your training and development needs.
