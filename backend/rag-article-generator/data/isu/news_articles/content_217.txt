#ShortTrackSkating
Following the completion of the ISU World Junior Short Track Speed Skating Championships in Dresden, GER, on January 27-29, 2023, the initial allocation of Winter Youth Olympic Games 2024 quota places has been concluded.



From 36 ISU Members participating at the ISU World Junior Short Track Championships in Dresden, 29 ISU Members earned an entry quota for the YOG 2024 in Gangwon, KOR, among them 4 ISU Members earned their first YOG quota, Brazil, Hong Kong, Slovenia and Turkey!
