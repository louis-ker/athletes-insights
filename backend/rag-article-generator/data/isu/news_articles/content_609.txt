The second stop of the ISU World Cup Short Track begins Friday 9 November. Don’t miss any of the action, including the first day of qualifying rounds, which will be streamed live on Skating ISU’sYouTube. Make sure you subscribe to our channel to receive live stream notifications.

The live action will continue on Saturday November 10 and Sunday November 11 with more exclusive World Cup sessions. Some geo-restrictions will apply on YouTube for Days 2 and 3 of Salt Lake City, but if you find yourself unable to watch the stream, check out one of the following channels to get access:
SBSfor those watching from the Republic of Korea.
ISU Skating Channelfor everyone else, where you can also watch for free.
The full schedule for ISU World Cup Short Track Salt Lake City is outlined below, clear your agenda and make time to enjoy the fastest sport on ice!
Feel the speed of Short Track. This season, keep #OneHandDown.
