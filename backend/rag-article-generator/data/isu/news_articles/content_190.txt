The ISU is releasing the entry quotas, qualifying times and details for ISU Short Track Speed Skating Championships for the season 2023/24.
In this communication you will find Entry Quotas for the following Championships:
-ISU Four Continents Short Track Speed Skating Championships 2024
-ISU European Short Track Speed Skating Championships 2024
-ISU World Junior Short Track Speed Skating Championships 2024
-ISU World Short Track Speed Skating Championships 2024
As well as the Qualifying Times & Seeding Times for ISU Short Track Speed Skating Championships 2024.

ENTRY DEADLINES
In accordance with Rule 295, paragraphs 4), the number of Competitors, substitutes, coaches and Team leaders shall be entered at least six (6) weeks before the first day of the Competition. The number of other personnel travelling with the team shall also be communicated separately at least six (6) weeks before the ISU Event concerned.ISU Members are seriously requested to enter only the number of Skaters they actually intend to send to the ISU Championships concerned. The names of the Competitors and substitutes names per category with their ISU Registration Number, the names of the Competitors for each of the respective individual distances and the confirmation of participation in the Relay shall be communicated to the organizing committee at the latest on the 10th day before the start of the first race of the Competition. For Post Entries Rule 115, paragraph 6 of the General Regulations applies.The exact deadlines are issued in the Official Announcement for each ISU Championships.
