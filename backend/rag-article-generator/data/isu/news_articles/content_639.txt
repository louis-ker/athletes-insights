Charles Hamelinwon gold in themen's 1500 metresat the 2018 World Championships in Montreal, Canada, zipping across the finish line to thunderous approval from the home crowd.

Charles Hamelin (CAN)
In a tight race led most of the way by Olympic gold medallistLim Hyo Jun(KOR), Quebec native Hamelin bided his time before making his move with two laps to go to win his10th ISU World Short Track Speed Skating Championships gold and 33rd medal overall.

Fellow Canadian,Samuel Girard, was penalized in the A Final for impeding another skater, whileHungary’sShaolin Sandor Liustayed was in medal contention for much of the race until a late push bySemen Elistratovmoved the Russian into the bronze position.

Defending championSeo Yi Ra(KOR) missed out on the A Final after coming in fourth in his semi-final heat and finished second in the B Final behind China’s Ren Ziwei.
