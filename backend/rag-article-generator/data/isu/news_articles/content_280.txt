The International Skating Union (ISU) has published the International Speed Skating and Short Track Speed Skating Competitions for the season 2022/23.

@International Skating Union
You will find the list of International Speed Skating and Short Track Speed Skating Competitions for the season 2022/23 in theISU Communication 2498.
This Communication does not replace announcements according to Rule 112. The International Competitions calendar is valid as of July 31, 2022. Due to the current pandemic, new International Competitions, cancellations, postponements or changes of venues might happen any time. All changes will be reflected in the ISU event calendar available on www.isu.org/events
International Competitions in Russia and Belarus are not listed but may be added if Communication No. 2469 is revoked.
