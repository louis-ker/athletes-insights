The ISU European Short Track Speed Skating Championships 2016 will be held in Sochi (RUS) at the Olympic venue “Iceberg Skating Palace”. The venue formerly chosen to host this Event (Megasport Arena in Moscow) is currently under reconstruction and will not be ready in time to host the Event.
The dates remain unchanged, i.e. January 22-24, 2016 and the Russian Skating Union is currently preparing the Official Announcement which should be available soon.
