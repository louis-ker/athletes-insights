ISU World Cup Short Track Speed Skating (GER) 2019©International Skating Union (ISU)
This season’s ISU World Cup Short Track Speed Skating will come to an end this weekend in Torino, Italy.
Find out who will be crowned the World Cup champions at the final leg of this thrilling event.
The event will be streamed live on theISU Skating YouTube page- including the first day of qualifying rounds on Friday February 8, which will be available to all fans worldwide.
Make sure you subscribe to the channel to receive live stream notifications.
The live action will continue on Saturday February 9 and Sunday February 10 with more exclusive World Cup sessions. Some geo-restrictions will apply on YouTube for Days 2 and 3 of Turin, but if you find yourself unable to watch the stream, check out one of the following channels to get access:
SBSfor those watching from the Republic of Korea.
ISU Skating Channelfor everyone else, where you can also watch for free.
The full schedule for ISU World Cup Short Track Torino is outlined below; clear your agenda and make time to enjoy the fastest sport on ice!
Feel the speed of Short Track. This season, keep #OneHandDown.
