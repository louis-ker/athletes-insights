Media accreditations for the ISU Short Track Speed Skating Skating Championships are open. Listed in the document on the right are the media accreditation contacts, deadlines and instructions for each ISU Short Tarck Speed Skating Championships.
All media wishing to attend ISU Championships must apply for media accreditation through theISUOnline Media Accreditation System (O.M.A.S).
Accreditation will be granted to professional journalists and photographers only and any incomplete applications will be refused.

The total number of accredited media representatives will be subject to space available. The Organizing Committee (OC) and ISU reserve the right to refuse accreditation should the number of applications exceed given space or if any requested information / documentation has not been provided in due time.
Further details are available on theISU Media Accreditationsection of the ISU website.
