#ShortTrackSkating


ISU Communication No. 2586 about Protection Equipment in Speed Skating and Short Track Speed Skating defines the minimal safety requirements (Safety Standards) and minimal standards to ensure fairness of competition (Level Playing Fields Standards) applicable to the Equipment (racing suits, underwear suits, helmets, gloves, eyewear and blades) in relation to Short Track Speed Skating and Team Events & Mass Start for Speed Skating. This document replaces ISU Communication No. 2400.
