#ShortTrackSkating
Don’t miss any of the action from the season’s thrilling ISU World Cup Short Track events – including the first day of qualifying rounds which will be streamed live to all fans on theISU Skating YouTube pagewithout any geo restriction. Make sure you subscribe to the channel to receive live stream notifications.
The live action will continue with the World Cup sessions on Saturdays and Sundays. Some geo-restrictions will apply on YouTube for Days 2 and 3, but if you find yourself unable to watch the stream, check out one of the channels below to get access.
