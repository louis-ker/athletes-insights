Media are informed that the media accreditations for the KPN ISU World Short Track Speed Skating Championships 2017 which take place in Rotterdam (NED) on March 10-13, 2017 are now open.
All accreditations are to be done through the ISU Online Media Accreditation system which is accessible through theMedia Accreditationsection of the website.The deadline for application isMarch 1, 2017and media must respect the deadline. Only professional media will be accredited.  The media information for the Event are also available in theMedia AccreditationSection.
