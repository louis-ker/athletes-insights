For the first time in Short Track history, every World Cup qualifying event will be available to watch worldwide on ISU Facebook and YouTube.
The qualifying rounds will be streamed live onFacebookandYouTubeon Friday November 2, so make sure you follow or subscribe to our pages in order to receive a notification once the live stream starts.
There will be geo-restrictions in place for the World Cup sessions on Saturday November 3 and Sunday November 4, but don’t worry we’ve got you covered – you just need to know where to find the live stream.
If you’re in the Republic of Korea visitSBS’ page for information. If you can’t find the live stream onFacebookorYouTube, head over to theISU Skating Channelwhere you can watch for free
The full schedule for ISU World Cup Short Track Calgary is outlined below, so you can make time to enjoy the fastest sport on ice. Feel the speed of Short Track. This season, keep#OneHandDown.
