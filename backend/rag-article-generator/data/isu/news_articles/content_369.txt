Following the online voting by ISU Members in June 2021, the Special Regulations & Technical Rules Short Track Speed Skating have been updated to reflect the accepted proposals and have been published on the ISU website.
The Drafting Committee for the 2021 Special Regulations & Technical Rules Short Track Speed Skating had the following composition:
Nathalie Lambert            Chair Short Track Speed Skating TCReinier Oostheim           Member Short Track Speed Skating TCWim de Deyne               Member Short Track Speed Skating TCHugo Herrnhof                ISU Sports Technical Director Speed SkatingFabrice Prahin                ISU Sports Manager Speed Skating
Printed copies of The Special Regulations will be available in due course.
