#WCSpeed
TheISU World Cup Speed Skating serieswill begin on November 15 in Minsk (BLR). It is comprised of five international events plus theISU World Cup Speed Skating Final, held on March 7 - 8 in Heerenveen (NED).
Listed in the document on the right are the media accreditation contacts, deadlines and instructions for each ISU World Cup Speed Skating event.
