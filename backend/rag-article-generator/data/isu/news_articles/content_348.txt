#ShortTrackSkating
TheISU World Cup Short Trackcontinues Thursday, October 27, 2021 in Nagoya, Japan. Don’t miss any of the action as the Skaters compete for their Olympic spots at the remaining ISU World Cup Short Track Olympic qualification events, which will be streamed live on theSkating ISU YouTube Channel. Make sure you subscribe to the channel to receive live stream notifications.

The qualification rounds and repechage races will not be geoblocked however some geo-restrictions will apply on the YouTube live stream for the World Cup sessions that take place on Saturday and Sunday afternoon. If you find yourself unable to watch the stream, check out one of the channels below to get access. Best efforts will be used to communicate any changes and updates to the list of geo-restricted territories. For precise details of broadcaster coverage please check local listings.
