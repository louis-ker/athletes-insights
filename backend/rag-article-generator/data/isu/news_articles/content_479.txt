#ShortTrackSkating
The ISU Short Track Speed Skating Championships will be streamed live to fans on theISU Skating YouTube page. Make sure you subscribe to the channel to receive live stream notifications. Some geo-restrictions will apply but if you find yourself unable to watch the stream, check out one of the channels below to get access.
The Championships season starts with the ISU Four Continents Short Track Speed Skating Championships in Montréal, Canada from January 10 – 12 and continues with the ISU European Short Track Speed Skating Championships, the ISU World Junior Short Track Speed Skating Championships and will end with the ISU World Short Track Speed Skating Championships.
Best efforts will be used to communicate any changes and updates to the list of geo-restricted territories. This list will be updated for each ISU Short Track Speed Skating Championships taking place in 2020. For precise details of broadcaster coverage please check local listings.
