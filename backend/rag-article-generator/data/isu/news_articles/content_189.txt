The International Skating Union (ISU) has published the International Speed Skating and Short Track Speed Skating Competitions for the season 2023/24.

@International Skating Union
The full list of International Speed Skating and Short Track Speed Skating Competitions for the season 2023/24 in theISU Communication 2582.
This Communication does not replace announcements according to Rule 112. The International Competitions calendar is valid as of July 31, 2023. In case of new International Competitions and, cancellations or postponements of announced International Competitions, all changes will be reflected in the ISU event calendar available on www.isu.org/events
International Competitions in Russia and Belarus are not listed but may be added ifCommunication No. 2469is revoked.
