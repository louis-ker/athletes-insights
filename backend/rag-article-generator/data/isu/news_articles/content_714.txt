The Audi ISU World Cup Short Track Speed Skating series will start on September 28, 2017. The Audi ISU World Cup Short Track Speed Skating series is comprised of four international events which are all Olympic qualifying events for the 2018 PyeongChang Olympic Winter Games.
On theMedia Accreditationssection of the website you will find all the relevant documents and information on how to apply for each event, including a list of media accreditation contacts, deadlines and instructions regarding the media accreditation process. All accreditations are to be done through theISU Online Media Accreditation System (OMAS).
