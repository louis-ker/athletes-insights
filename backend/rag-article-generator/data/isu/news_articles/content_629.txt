The ISU has decided to hold a course for ISU Starters in Short Track Speed Skating, which will be organized by the ISU Short Track Speed Skating Technical Committee in:
Oberstdorf, Germany on August 9-11, 2018
Program:
- Rule changes following the 2018 ISU Congress- ISU General Regulations, Special Regulations and Technical Rules for Short Track Speed Skating- Practice Starts at Oberstdorf Ice Rink
On August 10-11, 2018, all attending Starters will officiate during the International Competition "Oberstdorf Summercamp Trophy". A written examination will be held during the course to test the participants’ knowledge of the Regulations (see Rule 289, paragraph 6 b). The course will be conducted in English.
- Location- Participants- Transports- Visa requirements- Expenses and financial contributions- Other conditions
Please send your entries directly to the address indicated in this ISU Communication, giving exact information about the arrival and departure of the participantsbefore May 25, 2018.
